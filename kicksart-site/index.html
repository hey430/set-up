How ordering works

No card details on this site. Your order goes straight to our team on WhatsApp.

  1. Pick your pair

    Choose a colourway and your EU size, then add it to your cart. Mix as many pairs as you like.

  2. Place it on WhatsApp

    Fill in your delivery details and tap Place order. WhatsApp opens with your full order written out. Send it, and we confirm stock and delivery.

  3. Pay your way

    Pay the full amount when we confirm, or pay half now and the other half when your order arrives.

Questions

How does half and half work?

Choose “50 / 50” in your cart. You pay half of the total when we confirm your order on WhatsApp, and the other half when the order is delivered to you.

Is my order confirmed when I send the message?

Not yet. Our team replies on WhatsApp to confirm your size is in stock, the delivery charge for your city, and how to pay. Your order is confirmed once the first payment is received.

How do I pick my size?

The shoe fits true to size in EU sizing. If you are between sizes or have wide feet, go half a size up, or message us on WhatsApp and we will help.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 3–5 working days after confirmation. We share tracking on WhatsApp.

Can I exchange a size?

Yes, within 7 days of delivery if the pair is unworn and in its box. Message us on WhatsApp to arrange it.
