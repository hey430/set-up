Case Study: How Pete's Pasta Built a TikTok Shop Affiliate Engine

  • Aug 18, 2026
  • 2 min read
  • Haul House Team

Pete's Pasta partnered with Haul House for a six-month TikTok Shop build-out from April to September 2025. The focus was affiliate growth: getting the product into the hands, kitchens and videos of as many of the right creators as possible, and turning that content into sales.

The challenge

Food is one of the most competitive categories on TikTok Shop. Shoppers can't taste through a screen, so the content has to do the work: showing the product cooked, plated and enjoyed in a way that makes viewers want it for dinner tonight. Pete's Pasta needed to build reach and sales inside TikTok Shop, and to do it at a scale no brand account could reach alone.

Our approach

We ran the engagement through our standard four-stage process, with affiliate growth at the centre.

1. Discovery & brief

We started with the product and the audience: who buys pasta on TikTok Shop, what they respond to, and which product moments are most compelling on camera. That became the core angle and the brief every creator worked from.

2. Scripting & hooks

Food content lives or dies on the first frame. We built hooks around the most appetising visual moments and gave creators flexible structures they could adapt to their own style, from quick weeknight-dinner videos to family reactions and recipe walkthroughs. (More on that in our hook guide.)

3. Affiliate recruitment at scale

The heart of the program was affiliate growth. Over six months the program reached 19.18K affiliates, creators able to promote Pete's Pasta through their own shoppable content. We focused on creators whose audiences already cook and buy food online, and briefed them so the videos stayed on-message as volume grew.

4. Delivery & optimisation

As videos went live, performance data showed which creators, angles and formats were converting. We fed those learnings back into briefs so new affiliates started from what was already working.

The results

Across the six-month build-out, the program generated 10.6 million impressions and grew to a peak month of more than $65K in GMV, all through TikTok Shop.

Affiliate scale turns one brand story into thousands of creator stories, and each one is a storefront.

Key takeaways for food and CPG brands

  • Show, don't tell. In food, the demo is the pitch: the pour, the twirl, the first bite.
  • Scale comes from affiliates. Reach at this level comes from thousands of creators, not one account.
  • Briefs keep quality high at volume. The more creators you add, the more the brief matters.
  • Let data steer. Feed winning hooks and creators back into the program continuously.
