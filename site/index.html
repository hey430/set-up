FAQ

Questions Brands Ask Us

Can't find your answer? Email hey@socialbros.co and we'll reply.

What does a UGC and TikTok Shop growth agency actually do?

We handle the two things that move product on TikTok Shop: creators and creative. On the creator side we recruit, brief and manage affiliate creators who post shoppable videos about your product. On the creative side we script and produce UGC ads for TikTok, Meta and Instagram that you can run as paid media.

What is UGC, and why does it convert better than studio ads?

UGC (user-generated content) is video made by real creators in a native, first-person style: unboxings, try-ons, reviews and "hauls". It reads like a recommendation from a person rather than an ad from a brand, which is exactly what short-form feeds reward. Read our full guide to UGC ads.

Do you work with TikTok Shop affiliates?

Yes. Affiliate growth is a core service. We find creators whose audiences already buy in your category, brief them on hooks and product angles, and scale the ones who convert. Our Pete's Pasta program reached 19.18K affiliates. See our affiliate playbook.

Which platforms do you produce content for?

TikTok (organic, TikTok Shop and TikTok ads), Meta (Facebook and Instagram ads) and Instagram Reels. Each platform gets its own edit, pacing and specs rather than one video resized for all of them.

What kinds of brands are a good fit?

Consumer products that can be shown, tried or unboxed on camera, such as food, beauty, wellness, home and lifestyle goods, and that sell (or want to sell) through TikTok Shop or e-commerce.
